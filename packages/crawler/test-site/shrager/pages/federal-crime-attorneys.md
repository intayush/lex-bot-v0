# Federal Crimes Criminal Attorney, Pittsburgh, PA

In most criminal cases, the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania will dictate the law. However, some crimes are also tried under the jurisdiction of the U.S. Federal government. The U.S. Federal government has the ability to make arrests and claim jurisdiction in some cases.

Convictions for federal crimes often carry harsh penalties with prison sentences that far exceed those of state or local charges. It’s therefore imperative to carefully select a criminal attorney who *routinely handles cases in federal courts*.

## Offenses that go from a state to a federal charge include:

* Offenses that cross state lines, like kidnapping, **[drug trafficking](/drug-trafficking-attorneys "drug trafficking")** or [**internet pornography**](https://shragerdefense.com/internet-sex-crimes/ "internet sex crimes attorney").

* Any [**fraud offense**](/fraud-attorneys) that uses the U.S. Post Office, such as mail fraud.
