## Penalties for Retail Theft (Shoplifting) in Pennsylvania

If you or your child has been arrested for shoplifting as a juvenile delinquent, he or she may face different penalties and be eligible for other optional penalties. Please talk to an experienced [**juvenile defense attorney**](https://shragerdefense.com/juvenile-criminal-attorney/ "juvenile defense lawyer") about your rights.

**When Shoplifting is a Summary Offense:**\
The penalty for a summary offense in Pennsylvania is up to 90 days in jail and a $300 fine.

Shoplifting is considered a summary offense when:

* It is a first-offense, AND
* The value of the stolen item is less than $150.

**When Shoplifting is a Misdemeanor:**

The penalty for a first degree misdemeanor in Pennsylvania is up to five years in prison and $10,000 fine.

Shoplifting is considered a first degree misdemeanor when:

* The stolen item has a greater value than $150

The penalty for a second degree misdemeanor in Pennsylvania is up to two years in jail and $5,000 fine.

Shoplifting is considered a second degree misdemeanor when:

* It is your second offense for shoplifting, AND
* The value of the stolen item is LESS than $150

**When Shoplifting is a Felony:**

The penalty for a third degree felony in Pennsylvania is up to seven years in prison and a $15,000 fine.

Shoplifting is considered a third degree felony when:

* The item stolen is valued at over $2,000
* This is your third offense or subsequent offense
* The item stolen is a motor vehicle (car/motorcycle)
* The item stolen is a gun or firearm
