## HOMICIDE: DEFINITIONS, CHARGES AND SENTENCING IN PENNSYLVANIA

### [First Degree Murder](http://www.legis.state.pa.us/WU01/LI/LI/CT/HTM/18/00.025.002.000..HTM)

When an intentional killing is carried out it is considered a first-degree murder. This is the only type of murder punishable by the death penalty.

A first degree murder conviction is punishable by either mandatory life imprisonment or death. It also is punishable by a maximum $50,000 fine.

### [Second Degree Murder](http://www.legis.state.pa.us/WU01/LI/LI/CT/HTM/18/00.025.002.000..HTM)

Second-degree murder is defined by someone acting out a killing or being an accomplice to a killing while engaging in another felony.

A second degree murder conviction is punishable by a mandatory life term in prison. It is also punishable by a maximum $50,000 fine.

### [Third Degree Murder](http://www.legis.state.pa.us/WU01/LI/LI/CT/HTM/18/00.025.002.000..HTM)

All other kinds of murder fall into third-degree murder. It is a first-degree felony and is punishable by 20 to 40 years in prison. Also punishable by a maximum $50,000 fine.

### [Voluntary Manslaughter](http://www.legis.state.pa.us/WU01/LI/LI/CT/HTM/18/00.025.003.000..HTM)

If a person kills a human being with no prior intent and acted in “the heat of passion”, among other circumstances, this is voluntary manslaughter.

Voluntary manslaughter is a first-degree felony and is punishable by 10 to 20 years in prison and a maximum $25,000 fine.

### [Involuntary Manslaughter](http://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM\&ttl=18\&div=0\&chpt=25)

This typically refers to an unintentional killing of a human being caused by recklessness or criminal negligence or during the course of committing another crime such as **[DUI](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/)**.

If the victim of involuntary manslaughter was either a law enforcement officer or a child under the age of 12, sentencing is from five to 10 years and includes a fine of up to $25,000. Otherwise, sentencing is from two years and six months up to five years in prison and a fine of up to $10,000.

## PROVING GUILT IN PENNSYLVANIA HOMICIDE CASES

A person charged with murder is presumed innocent until proven guilty. The “burden of proof” is on the prosecutor who must prove criminal intent beyond a reasonable doubt in order to get a conviction.

A skilled defense attorney can counter the prosecutor’s evidence and theories by raising doubts as to the identity of the alleged murderer, motives, circumstantial evidence and other tangible evidence.

As an alleged homicide defendant, you have several rights. These include the right to examine and challenge any evidence brought against you, such as a weapon, motives and even the body. A skilled defense attorney will move to suppress evidence or have it excluded completely.

A strong and aggressive homicide defense attorney will fight to have your charges reduced or the case dismissed. Having charges reduced could mean avoiding decades in prison.

## DEFENSES AGAINST HOMICIDE CHARGES

Oftentimes murder is not a crime, for instance, if someone kills another person by acting in self-defense. It is also not illegal to kill a person unintentionally if not engaged in reckless behavior at the time of the accident.

Pleading insanity is another defense that can greatly reduce sentencing and keep defendants out of prison. Many people suffer mental health problems and may not have been in a normal state of mind when the incident occurred.

In the case of **[vehicular manslaughter](https://shragerdefense.com/criminal-lawyers-pittsburgh/vehicular-homicide/)**, a person may not have actually been at fault during an accident.

In addition, there are many instances where the police could have made an unlawful arrest, whereby, if proven, your case could be dismissed entirely.

Whatever is the case for your unique circumstances, our Pittsburgh homicide attorneys will work hard to find weaknesses in the prosecution’s argument in order to have your sentencing reduced or your charges dropped and the trial acquitted.
