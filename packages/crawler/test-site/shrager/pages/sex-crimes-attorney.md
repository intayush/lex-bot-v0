## **CONSEQUENCES OF SEX CRIMES CONVICTIONS IN PA**

Sex crime convictions can range from a second degree misdemeanor (up to two years in prison and a $5,000 fine) to a first degree felony (up to 20 years in prison and a $25,000 fine).

In addition, being registered as a sex offender requires that you list certain information about yourself and your whereabouts, making it very difficult to find employment and a place to live.

Under the Sexual Offender Registration and Notification Act (SORNA) sex offenders are required to register their whereabouts in each jurisdiction they live, work and go to school, in some cases for the rest of their lives.

**[Failure to register as a sex offender](https://shragerdefense.com/failure-to-register-sex-offender-pennsylvania/)** is a felony and can lead to prison time and thousands of dollars in fines.

Sex crimes could be punishable under both State and Federal Law. If you face federal charges, you will need an experienced **[Federal Crimes Attorney in Pittsburgh](https://shragerdefense.com/federal-crime-attorneys/)** on your side.
