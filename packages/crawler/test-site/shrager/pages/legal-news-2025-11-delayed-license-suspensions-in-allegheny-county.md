## Why This is Happening

The Allegheny County Department of Court Records stated that after conducting an internal audit in the spring of 2025, it discovered an error that had been preventing court documents from being forwarded to PennDOT. Under normal procedure, the Department sends a DL21 form to PennDOT after any conviction or plea that may result in a driver’s license suspension.

Because of this reporting failure, PennDOT did not receive the required documentation for a large number, but not all, of cases that date back to around 2013. The Department stated that it has no choice but to now send the paperwork to PennDot, which will issue license suspensions retroactively.
