# Pittsburgh Attorney Shelley Duff

Shelley Duff, Esq. is a Senior Attorney with Shrager Defense Attorneys where she exclusively practices [**Criminal Defense**](https://shragerdefense.com/criminal-lawyers-pittsburgh/). Shelley is a Pittsburgh native and graduate of the University of Pittsburgh and Cleveland Marshall College of Law.

![Shelley Duff - Pittsburgh Criminal Lawyer](https://www.shragerdefense.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Shelley-min.jpg)

Shelley started her legal career as an attorney with the Commonwealth of Kentucky’s Department of Public Advocacy. She later became a prosecutor with the Blair County Pennsylvania District Attorney’s Office and was an Assistant Public Defender for seven years with the Allegheny County Public Defender’s Office. She has extensive trial experience and has handled every type of case from DUI’s to homicides. Shelley is a member of the Pennsylvania and Ohio Bar, licensed in 1998 and 1996, respectively.

Shelley is also a professional musician. She is a vocalist and guitarist and can be found performing in various venues around the tri-state area.
