# Pittsburgh Attorney Kevin Chernosky

Attorney Kevin Chernosky is a criminal defense lawyer with Shrager Defense Attorneys in Pittsburgh, PA.

He was admitted to the Pennsylvania Bar in 2004 and began his legal career as an **Assistant Public Defender** in Franklin County, Pennsylvania.

![Kevin Chernosky - Pittsburgh Criminal Lawyer](https://www.shragerdefense.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/kevin-2025j.jpg)

He later became an **Assistant District Attorney in Allegheny County** where he prosecuted the most difficult and complex cases including countless violent felonies and child abuse cases as well as Death Penalty Cases on behalf of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.

He most recently held the position of **Deputy District Attorney of the Homicide Division** where he led and supervised a staff of homicide prosecutors while maintaining his own homicide trial calendar.

Additionally, Kevin was a special prosecutor for the Pittsburgh G-20 Conference in 2009, has handled complex investigations and death penalty cases, and has extensive experience with vehicular homicides.

Attorney Chernosky enjoys spending time with his wife and family, as well as his dog, Smalls, and cats, Gucci, Puffy, and Gravy.
