## FINES AND PENALTIES FOR A DUI IN PENNSYLVANIA

If you are under 21 and have been arrested for a DUI, read more about the consequences of an **[Underage DUI Arrest in Pennsylvania](/underage-dui-pennsylvania/ "Underage DUI in PA")**.

If you are over 21 many factors come into play. One of the most important being your Blood Alcohol Content (BAC) level, which will determine the severity of the penalties and fines you face.

Additionally, **[refusal of a breath of blood test](https://www.shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/refusing-blood-breath-tests/)** during a DUI arrest can lead to multiple license suspensions and increased criminal penalties.

**Pennsylvania has three tiers of BAC level which include:**

* **General impairment:** BAC of at least .08% but less than .10%, no accidents or injuries, driver is over the age of 21.
* **High Rate of Alcohol:** BAC of at least 0.10% but less than 0.16%; BAC of at least .08% but less than .10% with an accident or injury; school bus driver with BAC between 0.02% and .159% or commercial driver with BAC between .04 and 0.159%
* **Highest Rate of Alcohol**: Refusal to take a chemical test, BAC of .16% or higher, or drug use.

Depending on whether this is your **[first DUI offense](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/first-time-dui-pennsylvania/ "First Time DUI"), [second DUI](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/second-dui/)** or whether you have had **[multiple offenses](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/multiple-duis-in-pennsylvania/ "Multiple DUIs in Pennsylvania")**, your charges and penalties will vary.

## **First Time DUI Penalties**

As a first-time offender you face a minimum of a $300 fine and six months of probation, a misdemeanor on your record, as well as mandatory attendance of alcohol highway safety school and possible alcohol treatment and/or community service hours.

At a maximum you face a mandatory minimum incarceration period of 72 hours, a $1,000 to $5,000 fine, a two-year license suspension, and other requirements such as alcohol rehabilitation, attendance of alcohol highway safety school, and community service hours.

If this is your **[first DUI](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/first-time-dui-pennsylvania/)**, please read our question and answer guide for **[first time DUI offenses in Pennsylvania.](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/first-time-dui-pennsylvania/ "First Time DUI")**
