## PENALTIES FOR INTERNET CRIMES IN PENNSYLVANIA

Depending on the crime, the punishment can vary greatly. The only way to know for certain what types of penalties and sentencing you face if you are convicted is to contact an experienced Internet Crime Attorney.

**Maximum sentencing penalties could include:**

* Online Stalking: Up to five years in prison and fines of up to $10,000; Misdemeanor.
* Online Harassment: Up to one year in jail and fines up to $2,500; Misdemeanor.
* Computer Fraud: Up to seven years in jail and fines up to $15,000; Felony.
* Computer Theft: Up to seven years in jail and fines up to $15,000; Felony.
* Online Child Pornography: Up to seven years in jail and fines up to $15,000; Felony.
