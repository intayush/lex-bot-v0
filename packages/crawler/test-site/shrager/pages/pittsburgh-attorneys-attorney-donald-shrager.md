## Law Firm Founder

Attorney Donald Shrager received a Bachelor of Arts and Law degree from the University of Pittsburgh. After graduating from law school, Attorney Shrager was a law clerk for a distinguished jurist, Judge Olbum in Allegheny County. In 1967 Donald I. Shrager started his own law firm and it is still in its original location of the historic Frick Building in downtown Pittsburgh. He has been a member of the Allegheny County Bar Association since 1965.
