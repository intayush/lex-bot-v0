# Qualifying Questions

The chatbot should ask the following questions to qualify a lead. Questions marked as required must be asked before classification. Optional questions are asked if the conversation flows naturally.

## Required Questions

1. **What type of legal matter do you need help with?**
   - Helps route to correct practice area and attorney

2. **When did this issue first arise?**
   - Determines urgency and statute of limitations concerns

3. **What is your preferred method of contact?**
   - Collects contact information for follow-up

## Optional Questions

4. **Have you spoken with another attorney about this matter?**
   - Helps understand where the client is in their process

5. **What is your availability for a consultation?**
   - Streamlines scheduling

6. **Can you briefly describe what happened?**
   - Provides context for the attorney's initial review
