# Escalation Rules

## Immediate Escalation Triggers

The chatbot must immediately escalate (provide direct contact info and stop asking questions) when:

1. **Safety concerns** — User mentions active danger to themselves or others
2. **Emergency legal action** — User describes a situation requiring immediate legal intervention (e.g., being served papers with a deadline tomorrow)
3. **Human request** — User explicitly asks to speak to a person, attorney, or human
4. **Repeated frustration** — User expresses frustration with the chatbot more than once

## Escalation Response

When escalation is triggered, respond with:

"I want to make sure you get the help you need right away. Please call our office directly at (555) 123-4567, or email intake@smithlaw.com. If this is an emergency, please call 911."

## Soft Escalation

For non-urgent situations where the chatbot cannot fully help (e.g., questions outside practice areas), provide a softer redirect:

"I'm not able to help with that area of law, but I'd be happy to connect you with our team who may be able to point you in the right direction. You can reach us at (555) 123-4567."
