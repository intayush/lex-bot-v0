## WHAT IS VEHICULAR HOMICIDE OR HOMICIDE BY VEHICLE?

There are two types of vehicular homicide in Pennsylvania. The first is Homicide by Vehicle While DUI and the second is Homicide by Vehicle, a type of manslaughter.

**[Homicide by Vehicle](https://shragerdefense.com/criminal-lawyers-pittsburgh/vehicular-homicide/)** means unintentionally causing the death of another person by breaking a traffic law and engaging in reckless or grossly negligent operation of a motor vehicle.  It is a type of Manslaughter. This could mean running a stoplight and hitting a pedestrian, causing their death. It is a third degree felony.

A **Homicide by Vehicle While DUI&#x20;**&#x69;s a second degree felony. Not only will you be charged with a DUI, but you will also be charged with a “sentencing enhancement” of Homicide. If more than one person’s death was caused by your driving while DUI, you can be charged with multiple counts of homicide – one for every death.

## VEHICULAR HOMICIDE PENALTIES IN PENNSYLVANIA

**Homicide by Vehicle** is a third degree felony, punishable by up to seven years in prison, a $15,000 fine and a three year license suspension. The length of the prison sentence will largely depend on other DUI convictions, prior vehicular convictions and whether the defendant was on parole or probation at the time of arrest. Other factors like if the accident occurred in a work zone or involved an emergency vehicle can add time to the prison sentence.

A **Homicide by Vehicle While DUI** is covered by a special statute with harsher penalties. A DUI Vehicular Homicide conviction means a minimum of three years in jail and a maximum of ten years in prison for each person’s death, as well as a $25,000 fine for each individual death. It is a second degree felony.

## DEFENSE FOR VEHICULAR HOMICIDE

There are plenty of legal defenses that could be taken in an attempt to avoid long prison sentencing and fines or to avoid a conviction altogether.

In order for you to be convicted of **[Homicide by Vehicle](https://shragerdefense.com/criminal-lawyers-pittsburgh/vehicular-homicide/)** prosecutors must prove beyond a reasonable doubt these four elements:

* That you acted recklessly or were grossly negligent;
* That you did cause a death;
* That the death occurred while you were breaking a traffic law, and
* That breaking that law resulted in the death.

In order to be convicted of vehicular homicide it has to be proven that the driver was acting recklessly or in a grossly negligent manner. Having an experienced vehicular homicide attorney by your side is a critical step to disputing this element.

In order to be convicted of **Homicide by Vehicle While DUI** prosecutors must prove beyond a reasonable doubt these three elements:

* That you are guilty of DUI, or driving under the influence of alcohol or a controlled substance;
* That you did cause a death;
* That the death was a result of driving under the influence
