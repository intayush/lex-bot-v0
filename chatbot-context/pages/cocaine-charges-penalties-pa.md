## Simple Cocaine Possession Charges in PA

If you are charged with Simple Possession of a Controlled Substance, this means that there was no intent to deliver the cocaine (manufacture, sell, distribute or traffic).

Simple Possession is typically considered a misdemeanor under PA law and is punishable by up to a year in prison and a fine of up to $5,000 for a first offense.

In addition your driver’s license may be suspended or revoked. If this is your second offense for simple possession, your punishment could be much worse upon conviction.

## Possession with Intent to Deliver (PWID) Charges – Cocaine

Depending on the amount of cocaine and several other factors, such as whether you were found with a large amount of cash on your person, you may be charged with **[Possession with Intent to Deliver (PWID)](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-pwid-lawyer/)**, which is considered a felony upon conviction and comes with severe penalties and fines.

If you or loved one have been charged with PWID, it is because the arresting officers suspected you of:

* [**Drug Manufacturing**](drug-manufacturing-attorney-pennsylvania/ "manufacture drugs lawyer")
* [**Drug Distribution**](drug-distribution-attorneys "Drug distribution")
* [**Drug Trafficking**](drug-trafficking-attorneys)

PWID Cocaine is a felony punishable by imprisonment not exceeding ten years, or a fine not exceeding one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000), or both. This fine can be increased to exhaust the profits obtained from the illegal activity.

## Penalties & Sentencing of Cocaine Possession in PA\*

**First Offense:**

* Less than 2 grams: A felony, one year in prison and a $5,000 fine. Sentencing highly depends on prior convictions and other circumstances.
* More than 5 grams of Crack Cocaine: Felony, minimum five years in prison.
* 2 to 10 grams: Felony, minimum of one year in prison.
* Over 10 to 100 grams: Felony, three years of prison.
* 100 grams or more: Felony, four years of prison.

**Second Offense:**

* 2 to 10 grams: Felony, minimum of three years in prison
* Over 10 to 100 grams: Felony, minimum of five years of prison
* 100 grams or more: Felony, minimum of seven years of prison

**\***&#x50;enalties can be greater if this is your second or subsequent offense, or if you are found with drugs in a school zone, selling drugs to a minor or carrying an illegal weapon.
