## Penalties for Violent Crimes in Pennsylvania

The penalties and fines if you are convicted of a violent crime in Pennsylvania will largely depend on the nature of the crime and whether this is your first, second or multiple offense. Multiple offenders will receive much harsher punishments.

To fully understand what penalties you face, you should speak with an experienced violent crimes attorney, like Attorney Shrager who provides free consultations. Below are some general guidelines for sentencing if you are convicted of the following:

**• Summary Offense:** Up to 90 days of jail time and a fine of $300.\
**• Third Degree Misdemeanor:** Up to one year of jail time and a $2,000 fine.\
**• Second Degree Misdemeanor:** Up to two years of jail time and a $5,000 fine.\
**• First Degree Misdemeanor:** Up to five years of jail time and a $10,000 fine.\
**• Third Degree Felony:** Up to seven years of prison time and a $15,000 fine.\
**• Second Degree Felony:** Up to ten years of prison time and a $25,000 fine.\
**• First Degree Felony:** Up to 20 years of prison time and a $25,000 fine
