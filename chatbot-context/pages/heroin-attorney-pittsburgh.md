## Possession of Heroin in PA – Charges, Penalties & Sentencing

**Simple Possession of Heroin (less than one gram):**

* Misdemeanor, one year in prison, first offense.
* Misdemeanor, three years in prison, second or subsequent offense.

**Buying Heroin:**

* Misdemeanor, three years in prison for a first offense.

**Possession with Intent to Deliver:**

* Felony; maximum imprisonment of 15 years, or a fine not exceeding two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000), or both. This fine can be increased to exhaust the profits obtained from the illegal activity.

## WHAT A HEROIN ATTORNEY CAN DO FOR YOU

A skilled [**heroin attorney**](pittsburgh-drug-lawyer "drug attorney pennsylvania") will look at all of the alleged facts in your case. In order for you to be arrested there had to have been probable cause. If it was found that your arrest stemmed from an illegal search, your charges will be dismissed.

Oftentimes people who are charged with heroin possession are struggling with addiction. An aggressive attorney may be able to persuade the District Attorney to allow you to enter a drug treatment program instead of serving in prison.

Options exist like **The Allegheny County Drug Court Program**, which is a rehabilitative program that can shorten your sentencing.

The **Probation without Verdict Program (PWV)** is another option which could allow your charges to be dropped completely and your criminal record expunged.

It is important to have an aggressive and experienced attorney on your side to fight for your acceptance into these programs.
