# Multiple DUIs in PA

If you have been arrested more than once for DUI, you will need to understand your rights fully and begin building your **[DUI defenses](https://shragerdefense.com/dui-defense-pennsylvania/)** as soon as possible.

[**DUI Penalties in Pennsylvania**](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/pennsylvania-dui-laws/) are some of the toughest in the nation and the consequences can be devastating for defendants and their families. Heavy fines and a prison sentence along with license suspension are only a few of the consequences of multiple DUI convictions.

In many instances DUIs are charged as **[felonies](https://www.shragerdefense.com/felony-dui-in-pennsylvania/)** when an individual has multiple prior convictions.

Additionally, new DUI laws became effective November 2022 which increase the grading and penalties for certain multiple DUI offenders.
