## CONSEQUENSES OF A DOMESTIC VIOLENCE CHARGE IN PA

Directly following a domestic violence arrest, after you are released from a probable night in jail, you may not be able to return to your home or have any contact with the accuser.

Typically domestic violence convictions result in a misdemeanor, but depending on the crime and if any injuries were sustained you could be charged with a felony.

In addition to jail time and heavy fines, a conviction could also mean losing your custody rights to your children or losing your right to own a firearm. If you are a licensed professional, such as a doctor or accountant, you can also lose your right to practice your profession.

If your spouse or significant other takes out a [**Protection From Abuse (PFA) Order**](/protection-from-abuse-order-pennsylvania "pfa filed in pa"), sometimes known as a restraining order, you face criminal contempt charges. The penalties are severe if you violate the order and can include jail time, hefty fines, probation and community service. A PFA in Pennsylvania means no physical contact, but also means no phone calls, texting or emails.
