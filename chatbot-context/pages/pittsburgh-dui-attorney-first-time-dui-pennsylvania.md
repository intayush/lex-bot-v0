## FIRST TIME DUI IN PA – QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Below are answers to several common questions about first time DUI or DWI charges.

**Q. What is the percentage of blood alcohol content (BAC) that makes it unlawful to drive a motor vehicle in PA?**

**A.** The first thing to note is that you do not have to be over the legal limit to be arrested for a DUI. If the police suspect you have been drinking, you can be charged with “general impairment.” Read **[PA DUI laws](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/pennsylvania-dui-laws/)** for more information.

Adults cannot legally operate a motor vehicle with a BAC of .08% or greater. Minors cannot legally operate a motor vehicle while their BAC is .02% or greater. If operating a commercial vehicle Commercial Truck Drivers cannot legally operate a motor vehicle at a BAC of .04% or greater. **Read more about****[ underage drinking penalties](/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/underage-dui-pennsylvania/ "Underage Drinking") and [DUI with a CDL license](https://shragerdefense.com/dui-cdl-license-pennsylvania/).**

**Q. What is considered a first offense or “prior” offense in PA?**

A first offense means that you do not have a prior conviction for DUI in PA.

A prior offense is a DUI conviction that occurred within **10 years prior** to the current DUI arrest.

Additionally, it can also refer to a DUI conviction that took place before the sentencing for the current DUI arrest, regardless of the time frame.

**Q. What happens if I refused to take a breath or blood alcohol test?**

**A.** Your license will be [**suspended**](https://www.shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/refusing-blood-breath-tests/) for 12 months by PennDOT and you will be subject to the [**highest tier of DUI penalties**](#t3) if you refused breath or blood testing pursuant to a valid search warrant (this does not include a portable roadside breath test). However, refusing to take the test does not mean your case is hopeless. There are many strategies to defend a DUI case.

**Q. Will my drivers license be suspended?**

As said above, if you refused chemical testing you will receive a [**civil license suspension**](https://www.shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/refusing-blood-breath-tests/) by PennDOT for 12 months which will be in addition to any license suspension imposed if you are convicted. If you did not refuse, there is no license suspension unless you are convicted. If you are convicted, the potential duration of a license suspension can be 0-24 months depending upon the circumstances of your DUI.

## FIRST-TIME DUI PENALTIES IN PA

DUI penalties in Pennsylvania are controlled by [**75 Pa.C.S. § 3804**](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/WU01/LI/LI/CT/HTM/75/00.038.004.000..HTM).

## TIER 1 DUI OFFENSES (misdemeanor):

Tier 1 offenses are the least severe and include the following:

* **§3802(a)(1) – General Impairment – Rendered Incapable Of Safely Driving**: This offense involves driving under the influence to the point where you’re unable to drive safely.
* **§3802(a)(2) – General Impairment (BAC at least 0.08% but less than 0.10%)**: This offense involves driving with a blood alcohol concentration (BAC) that’s above the legal limit but less than 0.10%.

## TIER 1 FIRST-TIME DUI PENALTIES:

* A mandatory minimum term of six months’ probation;
* A fine of $300;
* Required attendance at an alcohol highway safety school;
* Compliance with all drug and alcohol treatment requirements;
* No license suspension

## TIER 2 DUI OFFENSES (misdemeanor):

Tier 2 offenses are more severe and include the following:

* **§3802(b) – High Rate Of Alcohol (BAC at least 0.10% but less than 0.16%)**: This offense involves driving with a BAC that’s at least 0.10% but less than 0.16%.
* **§3802(e) – Minors (Under age 21, BAC 0.02% or higher)**: This offense involves a minor driving with a BAC of 0.02% or higher.
* **§3802(f)(1)(i) – Operating a Commercial Vehicle (BAC 0.04% or greater)**: This offense involves operating a commercial vehicle with a BAC of 0.04% or greater.
* **§3802(f)(1)(ii) – Operating a School Bus (BAC 0.02% or greater)**: This offense involves operating a school bus with a BAC of 0.02% or greater.
* **§ 3804(b) – Accident involving injury, damage or death (BAC 0.08% but less than 0.10%)**: This offense involves causing an accident that results in injury, damage, or death while driving with a BAC of 0.08% but less than 0.10%.

## TIER 2 FIRST-TIME DUI PENALTIES:

* Imprisonment for not less than 48 consecutive hours;
* A fine of not less than $500 nor more than $5,000;
* Required attendance at an alcohol highway safety school;
* Compliance with all drug and alcohol treatment requirements;
* A 12-month suspension of operating privileges upon conviction, with a 1-year ignition interlock which can begin immediately.

## **TIER 3 DUI OFFENSES (misdemeanor):**

Tier 3 offenses are the most severe and include the following:

* **§1547(b)(2)(ii) – General Impairment DUI Conviction Plus Refusal of Breath or Blood Testing Pursuant to a Valid Search Warrant**: This offense involves a general impairment DUI conviction plus refusal of breath testing or blood testing pursuant to a valid search warrant (roadside breath tests not included).
* **§3802(c) – Highest Blood Alcohol (0.16% or higher)**: This offense involves driving with a BAC of 0.16% or higher.
* **§3802(d) – Controlled Substances**: This offense involves driving under the influence of controlled substances.

## TIER 3 FIRST-TIME DUI PENALTIES:

* Imprisonment for not less than 72 consecutive hours;
* A fine of not less than $1,000 nor more than $5,000;
* Required attendance at an alcohol highway safety school;
* Compliance with all drug and alcohol treatment requirements;
* A 12-month drivers license suspension (with an additional 12-18 months if you refused breath testing or blood testing pursuant to a valid search warrant), with a 1-year ignition interlock which can begin immediately.
