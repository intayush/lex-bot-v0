Usually if you have been **[charged with PWID in PA](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-pwid-lawyer/)** it is because there was a large amount of the drug found or under other circumstances, like the type of packaging of the drug or a large amount of cash found at the alleged crime scene.

## PENALTIES IN PA FOR DRUG TRAFFICKING

[**A PWID conviction**](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-pwid-lawyer/) is typically considered a felony and the penalty upon conviction can be up to 15 years of imprisonment, or a fine not exceeding two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000), or both. Also are court costs, loss of driver’s license and other losses of freedom, like being able to apply for federal student loans.

If this is your second or subsequent offense, actual sentencing is much harsher. The severity of the punishment largely depends on the type of drug involved.

To convict you of drug trafficking, a prosecutor must show beyond a reasonable doubt that you illegally obtained the drug and then delivered or intended to deliver it to another person.

In large-scale drug operations, and sometimes even in small ones, you will be tried by both the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and the U.S. Federal Government.

If you are being tried with drug trafficking by the U.S. Federal Government, you will need to hire an experienced and aggressive **[Federal Crime Attorney in Pittsburgh](federal-crime-attorneys "pittsburgh federal crime lawyer")**.

To see the penalties you face if charged with Federal Drug Trafficking, read the information on the [**Justice.gov website.**](http://www.justice.gov/dea/druginfo/ftp3.shtml "drug trafficking penalties")
