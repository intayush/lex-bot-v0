# DUI Drugs / Controlled Substance in Pennsylvania

A person arrested for **[DUI in Pennsylvania](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/pennsylvania-dui-laws/)** faces tough penalties. And if drugs or a controlled substance were found in your system Pennsylvania’s tough laws warrant even harsher punishment.

A DUI of drugs is punishable by the same standards of the highest level of DUI of alcohol.

**If you are convicted of a first time DUI Controlled Substance in Pennsylvania you face:**

* **Jail time (Up to 6 months)**
* **A suspended drivers’ license**
* **Heavy fines (Up to $5,000)**
* **A criminal record (A misdemeanor)**
* **Other consequences (getting insurance, getting a job, college admission)**

## DUI OF DRUGS / CONTROLLED SUBSTANCE

[**Pennsylvania DUI penalties**](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/pennsylvania-dui-laws/)**&#xA0;**&#x63;an be very tough.

The **[Commonwealth of Pennsylvania’s Controlled Substances, Drugs, Device and Cosmetic Act](https://shragerdefense.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/PA-Controlled-Substances-Drugs-Act.pdf)&#x20;**&#x6C;ists the drugs and controlled substances, which fall under different schedules.

Driving a vehicle while under the influence of certain drugs that fall under these schedules is not permissible. This could include **[prescription medication](https://shragerdefense.com/illegal-prescription-drugs-attorney/)**, **[marijuana](https://shragerdefense.com/marijuana-lawyer-pittsburgh/)**, **[cocaine](https://shragerdefense.com/cocaine-charges-penalties-pa/), [crystal meth](https://shragerdefense.com/pennsylvania-meth-crime-lawyer/)** or **[heroin](https://shragerdefense.com/heroin-attorney-pittsburgh/)**.

## Penalty for DUI of Drugs / Controlled Substance Convictions

Sentencing guidelines for DUI Controlled Substance convictions in Pennsylvania are listed below.

These guidelines do not list the indirect punishments you face if you are convicted of a DUI, which could include loss of employment, difficult finding future employment, difficulty in getting into college, loss of parental rights, difficulty finding housing and insurance, your reputation and other court fees and fines.

### First Offense – DUI Drugs in Pennsylvania

If you are convicted, a first time DUI of a controlled substance in PA comes with:

* a minimum of 72 hours and a maximum of 6 months in jail
* a misdemeanor on your record
* a fine of $1,000 to $5,000
* a one year license suspension.

Other penalties include attending alcohol highway safety school and other treatment programs. Read our page on a **[First DUI in PA](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/first-time-dui-pennsylvania/)**.

### Second Offense – DUI Drugs in Pennsylvania

A second drugged DUI conviction means you could face:

* a minimum of 90 days in prison and a **maximum of five years in prison**
* an 18 month license suspension
* a first degree misdemeanor
* a fine from $1,500 to $10,000
* alcohol highway safety school
* one year of the ignition interlock program.

See our page on a **[Second DUI in PA](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/second-dui/)**.

### Multiple Offenses – DUI Drugs in Pennsylvania

Being convicted more then twice in Pennsylvania is a very serious offense. You could face one to five years in prison, an 18 month license suspension, a first degree misdemeanor, fines ranging from $2,500 to $10,000, alcohol highway safety school and one year of the ignition interlock program.

See our page on **[Multiple DUIs in PA](https://shragerdefense.com/pittsburgh-dui-attorney/multiple-duis-in-pennsylvania/)**.
